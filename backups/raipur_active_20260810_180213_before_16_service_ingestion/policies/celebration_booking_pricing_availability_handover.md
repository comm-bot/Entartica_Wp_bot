# Celebration Booking, Pricing, Availability and Human Assistance

## Booking Enquiry

The chatbot may collect a celebration enquiry from guests. The following information may be collected:

- Customer name
- Contact number (received through the approved channel)
- Location: Entartica Sea World, Raipur
- Selected celebration service (Pontoon Celebration, Floating Gazebo, Jetty Gazebo, Houseboat Celebration, Party Boat Celebration)
- Occasion (birthday, anniversary, proposal, corporate event, family gathering, etc.)
- Preferred date
- Preferred time
- Number of guests
- Special requirements (decoration, food, music, photography, accessibility needs)

Collecting these details does not constitute a confirmed booking. All enquiries are passed to the Entartica team for follow-up and confirmation.

## Advance Booking Notice

Advance booking requirements vary by celebration service and current operational conditions. The Entartica team must confirm the applicable notice period for a specific enquiry.

## Booking Confirmation

The chatbot does not provide final booking confirmation. Final booking confirmation is issued only by the Entartica team through the approved process. Guests must not consider a celebration booking confirmed until they receive this official confirmation.

## Pricing

Current pricing for all celebration experiences and packages must be obtained from the approved Entartica pricing source or confirmed directly by the Entartica team. Pricing depends on the selected experience, group size, package inclusions, custom add-ons, and date selected. The chatbot does not quote, confirm, or guarantee any price.

## Live Availability

Current slot, date, vessel, gazebo, and guest-capacity availability for all celebration services must be verified through the approved live availability source or directly with the Entartica team. The chatbot does not confirm live availability. A celebration service being described in this knowledge base does not mean it is available on any specific date, time, or configuration.

## Package Inclusions

Celebration package inclusions — including decoration, food, cake, music, photography, and other add-ons — vary by experience and by the package selected. The chatbot does not confirm specific package inclusions. All inclusions must be confirmed in writing by the Entartica team during the booking process.

## Payment

The chatbot must not confirm payment based on a customer message, screenshot, or receipt. Payment confirmation must come from the approved Entartica payment system only. Guests must not share payment card details or sensitive financial information through the chatbot.

## Cancellation and Refund

Cancellation and refund requests require direct assistance from the Entartica team. The chatbot cannot process cancellations or issue refunds. Current cancellation and refund terms for celebration experiences must be confirmed with the Entartica team at the time of booking.

## Complaints

Complaints about celebration experiences require human assistance. The chatbot may acknowledge a complaint and collect the guest's name and contact number for follow-up by the Entartica team. The chatbot cannot resolve complaints.

## Special Requirements

Custom decoration setups, food and catering, cake arrangements, music, photography coordination, accessibility needs, overnight stay reservations, and other special event requirements cannot be confirmed through the chatbot. These items require direct human review and confirmation by the Entartica team.

## Human Handover

The following situations must be transferred to the Entartica team and cannot be resolved by the chatbot:

- Current pricing or package quotations for any celebration service
- Specific package inclusion confirmation
- Custom decoration, themes, and setups
- Food, catering, and cake arrangements
- Music and DJ requests
- Photography and event coordination
- Live date, slot, vessel, and gazebo availability
- Final booking confirmation
- Payment processing and payment confirmation
- Cancellation and rescheduling requests
- Refund requests
- Complaints and feedback
- Overnight stay reservations for the Houseboat Celebration
- Accessibility or special mobility requirements
- Any query involving an incident, injury, or emergency
- Any query where the chatbot cannot answer with confidence based on the approved knowledge base

When transferring to a human team member, the chatbot should collect the customer's name, contact number, preferred celebration service, and a brief description of the query before handover.
