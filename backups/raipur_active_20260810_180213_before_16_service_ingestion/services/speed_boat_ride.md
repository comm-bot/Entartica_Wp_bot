# Speed Boat Ride — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Speed Boat Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a high-energy passenger boat experience designed for guests who want to enjoy speed, movement, and views across the water without operating the boat themselves.

Guests ride together while an experienced captain operates the Speed Boat. The experience is focused on acceleration, fast-paced movement, excitement, and enjoying the surrounding lake from a faster-moving boat.

It is a good option for families, friends, and groups looking for a more thrilling water experience than a relaxed sightseeing cruise while still participating as passengers rather than drivers.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Offers a fast-paced passenger experience on the water.
- Guests enjoy the thrill without needing to operate the boat themselves.
- An experienced captain handles the vessel.
- Multiple Speed Boat configurations allow different group sizes.
- Combines adrenaline with views of the surrounding water.
- Provides a more energetic experience than slower leisure boat rides.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests can expect to board a Speed Boat and experience fast movement across the water while an experienced captain operates the vessel.

The ride is designed around speed and excitement rather than calm cruising.

Entartica's official Speed Boat information also indicates that water spray can form part of the experience, so guests should expect a more active ride than a relaxed Pontoon Boat outing.

All applicable safety instructions must be followed before and during participation.

## Capacity

Entartica's current Speed Boat information publishes different capacities depending on the boat type.

Published configurations include:

- Bayliner 175: up to 7 guests
- Fomar Speed Boat: up to 6 guests
- Regal 2700: up to 14 guests
- Regal 19: up to 8 guests
- Catamaran Speed Boat: up to 6 guests

Overall published Speed Boat capacity therefore ranges from:

**6 to 14 guests, depending on the boat type**

The exact Speed Boat model operating at Raipur for a particular session is not established in this knowledge document.

## Duration

Typical ride duration:

**Approximately 5 to 10 minutes**

This is the approved duration for the Speed Boat Ride as a one-time access experience.

The current official Speed Boat page describes the activity as **one round per ride** rather than publishing the approved numeric duration. This does not replace the approved 5–10 minute value.

## Operating Hours

## Access Type

**One-Time Access**

Speed Boat Ride is a one-time Boat House experience.

One-time access refers to one ride/session under the applicable access arrangement and does not mean unlimited repeat use.

## Getting Wet

Some water spray or splashing may occur during the Speed Boat Ride because of the speed and movement of the boat.

The exact amount of splashing can vary with operating and water conditions and should not be treated as guaranteed.
