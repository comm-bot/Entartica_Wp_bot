# Jet Ski Ride — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Jet Ski Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a high-speed personal watercraft experience on Jhanjh Lake designed for guests who enjoy active, adrenaline-focused water activities.

Unlike passenger boat rides where guests simply sit back while someone else operates the vessel, the Jet Ski experience gives the rider direct control of the watercraft. Guests can experience acceleration, turning, speed, and movement across the water while following the designated riding instructions.

The experience can also be enjoyed by first-time riders. Entartica provides guidance for beginners and safety instructions before or during participation, allowing newcomers to understand the basic operation before enjoying the ride.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Gives the rider direct control of a high-powered personal watercraft.
- Combines speed, acceleration, turning, and open-water movement.
- Provides a more active experience than passenger-based Boat House rides.
- Designed around adrenaline and high-speed water recreation.
- First-time riders can receive guidance from Entartica staff.
- A single-rider format gives the participant an individual riding experience.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests can expect an active, self-driven water experience rather than a passenger ride.

Before riding, applicable operating and safety instructions are provided. The rider then operates the Jet Ski within the permitted activity area and follows all directions given by Entartica staff.

The ride involves high-speed movement on the water, including acceleration and manoeuvring.

## Capacity

The current official Entartica Jet Ski information states:

**1 rider per Jet Ski**

## Duration

Typical ride duration:

**Approximately 5 to 10 minutes**

This is the approved duration for the Jet Ski Ride as a one-time access experience.

## Operating Hours

Approved water-sports and ride operating hours:

**10:00 AM to 6:30 PM**

All timings remain subject to weather and operational conditions.

## Access Type

**One-Time Access**

The Jet Ski Ride is a one-time access water-sports experience.

One-time access refers to one ride/session under the applicable access arrangement and does not mean unlimited repeat use.

## Getting Wet

Jet Ski is a water-based, high-speed activity where splashing may occur during the experience.

Guests should use suitable water-activity clothing and follow any applicable attire instructions provided for their ride or access type.

## Safety Information

A life jacket is required for the Jet Ski experience.

Entartica's Raipur Jet Ski information also describes a safety cut-off mechanism connected to the rider. If the rider loses balance and falls from the Jet Ski, the mechanism is designed to stop the engine.

Entartica states that instructors are available to guide beginners and assist when required.

Guests must follow all operating and safety instructions provided by Entartica staff.

## Participation Requirements

Entartica's official Rules & Regulations state that Jet Ski participation is not allowed for:

- Pregnant women
- People with heart problems

The current approved information does not establish a definitive Jet Ski-specific:

- Minimum age
- Maximum age
- Height requirement
- Weight limit

No previous Jet Ski riding experience is required for beginners to receive guidance.

## Swimming Requirement

A definitive Jet Ski-specific swimming requirement is not currently established in this knowledge document.

The requirement to wear a life jacket should not be interpreted by itself as proof that swimming ability is or is not required.

## Dress Guidance

Entartica's Jet Ski information recommends comfortable swimwear and a life jacket for the activity.

Any additional clothing requirements should follow the applicable on-site water-sports instructions.

## How It Works

1. The rider joins the Jet Ski activity subject to applicable participation requirements.
2. Entartica staff provide the necessary operating and safety guidance.
3. The rider wears the required life jacket.
4. The rider operates the Jet Ski within the permitted activity area.
5. The experience involves acceleration, turning, and movement across the water.
6. The rider returns at the end of the allocated one-time ride session.
