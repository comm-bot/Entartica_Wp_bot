# Daycation Package — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Daycation Package at Entartica Sea World, Raipur combines daytime Club Room access at Mayfair Lake Resort with Entartica water activities, giving guests a same-day resort-and-water experience without an overnight stay.

Guests can take breaks and relax in the Club Room while enjoying unlimited access to H2O Play Park activities during the approved Daycation window. The package also includes one-time access to three different Boat House experiences: Jet Ski, Speed Boat, and Pontoon Boat.

This combination gives guests variety within one visit — recreational H2O activities, higher-energy Boat House experiences, a relaxed Pontoon Boat ride, resort comfort, and a food benefit at Chowpatty.

The Daycation Package is a good option for families, couples, friends, and small groups looking to spend an afternoon enjoying Entartica and Mayfair Lake Resort facilities without booking an overnight Staycation.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Combines Entartica water activities with daytime resort-room access.
- The Club Room provides a comfortable place to relax between activities.
- Unlimited H2O Play Park access allows guests to enjoy multiple recreational water activities during the package window.
- One-time access to Jet Ski, Speed Boat, and Pontoon Boat brings different Boat House experiences into the same package.
- A food voucher at Chowpatty gives guests an opportunity to take a food break during their visit.
- The package provides a same-day alternative for guests who want resort comfort and water activities without an overnight stay.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests receive daytime access to a Club Room at Mayfair Lake Resort together with access to Entartica Sea World activities during the approved Daycation period.

They can enjoy H2O Play Park activities on an unlimited-access basis, subject to the operating conditions and participation requirements of each individual activity.

The package also includes one-time access to:

- Jet Ski
- Speed Boat
- Pontoon Boat

A food voucher for Chowpatty is included in the currently published package configuration.

Guests should expect individual rides and activities to have their own safety and participation requirements.

## Package Inclusions

### Club Room Day Access

The Daycation Package includes daytime access to a Club Room at Mayfair Lake Resort.

The room provides guests with a space to relax during the Daycation and take breaks between activities.

### Unlimited H2O Play Park Access

The currently published Daycation configuration includes unlimited access to H2O Play Park.

Activities displayed for the Daycation include:

- Aqua Cycle
- Kayak
- Kids Pedal Boat
- Adult Bumper Boat
- Kids Bumper Boat
- Aqua Roller
- Water Bike
- Zorbing Ball

Unlimited H2O Play Park access applies during the valid Daycation period and remains subject to individual activity eligibility, queueing, weather, safety, maintenance, and operational conditions.

Unlimited access does not mean that one individual H2O activity can be used continuously for the full package period.

Individual H2O activity turn or session durations are not separately established by the Daycation Package.

### One-Time Boat House Access

The Daycation Package includes one-time access to each of:

- Jet Ski
- Speed Boat
- Pontoon Boat

Each of these is a separate Boat House experience with its own participation and safety requirements.

Additional or repeated Boat House access is not included in the standard one-time entitlement.

### Food Voucher

The currently published Daycation configuration includes a food voucher redeemable at Chowpatty during the applicable redemption period.

The current official website lists the redemption window as:

**3:00 PM to 6:00 PM**

Unused voucher value is stated as non-refundable and cannot be carried forward.

## Guest Limit

The dedicated official Daycation page currently describes Club Room/package access for:

**Up to 4 persons**

Additional guests may be possible under separate applicable terms.

A separate Entartica Offers page currently describes an Ultimate Daycation configuration for 3 guests. This discrepancy is recorded under FACTS TO VERIFY and does not change the currently retained 4-person configuration in this knowledge document.

## Duration

The Daycation Package is a same-day experience and does not include an overnight stay.

Approved Daycation access period:

**2:00 PM to 6:00 PM**

This timing represents the Daycation package window.

Individual included activities have their own activity format and should not be interpreted as running continuously for the complete four-hour Daycation period.

## Operating Hours

Approved Daycation operating hours:

**2:00 PM to 6:00 PM**

All timings remain subject to weather and operational conditions.

## Access Type

**Bundled Daycation Package**

The package combines:

- Club Room day access
- Unlimited H2O Play Park access
- One-time Boat House access
- Food voucher

It is not a standalone water ride or individual water-sport service.

## Safety Information

Guests must follow the applicable safety instructions for each water activity they participate in.

The official Daycation information states that life jackets must be worn during water rides.

Entartica also lists the following safety-related and guest-support facilities on the Daycation page:

- Safety jackets
- Rescue boats
- Safety tools
- First aid

Individual activities may have their own specific safety requirements.

## Participation Requirements

Eligibility for each included water activity depends on the participation requirements applicable to that individual service.

Access to the Daycation Package does not automatically establish that every guest is eligible for every included ride or activity.

Any age, weight, health, capacity, or other participation requirement should be taken from the relevant individual service knowledge document when confirmed.
