# Staycation Combo — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Staycation Combo at Entartica Sea World, Raipur combines an overnight stay at Mayfair Lake Resort with Entartica water activities, giving guests a resort-and-water getaway rather than a standalone ride or activity.

The currently published package includes a one-night Plush Club Room stay for two, complimentary breakfast for two, unlimited H2O Play Park access, and one-time access to selected Boat House experiences.

This combination allows guests to balance relaxation at Mayfair Lake Resort with recreational H2O activities and higher-energy Boat House experiences during the same overnight visit.

The Staycation Combo is a good option for couples, families, and leisure guests who want to extend their Entartica experience beyond a same-day visit.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Combines an overnight resort stay with Entartica water activities.
- A Plush Club Room provides a comfortable place to relax between activities.
- Complimentary breakfast for two adds a dining element to the overnight stay.
- Unlimited H2O Play Park access provides multiple recreational water activities.
- One-time access to Jet Ski, Speed Boat, and Pontoon Boat adds three different Boat House experiences.
- Guests can combine active water experiences with a more relaxed lakeside stay.
- Offers an overnight alternative to the same-day Daycation experience.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests receive a one-night stay at Mayfair Lake Resort together with access to Entartica Sea World activities under the applicable Staycation package.

The package currently includes:

- Plush Club Room stay for two
- Complimentary breakfast for two
- Unlimited H2O Play Park access
- One-time Jet Ski access
- One-time Speed Boat access
- One-time Pontoon Boat access

Guests can use their accommodation as a place to relax while enjoying Entartica activities during the applicable operating periods.

Each included water activity remains subject to its own safety, participation, weather, and operational requirements.

## Package Inclusions

### Accommodation

The currently published Staycation configuration includes:

**1-night Plush Club Room stay for 2 guests at Mayfair Lake Resort**

Official Entartica information positions the room stay around comfort and lake-side surroundings.

### Breakfast

The package currently includes:

**Complimentary breakfast for 2 guests**

Other meals are not established as standard Staycation inclusions in this knowledge document.

### Unlimited H2O Play Park Access

The Staycation Package includes unlimited access to H2O Play Park during the applicable activity operating period.

Current Staycation material displays H2O activities including:

- Kids Bumper Boat
- Aqua Roller
- Water Bike
- Zorbing Ball
- Aqua Cycle
- Kayak
- Kids Pedal Boat
- Adult Bumper Boat

Unlimited access does not mean continuous occupation or uninterrupted use of one individual activity.

Individual H2O Play Park activity turn or session durations are not separately confirmed.

### One-Time Boat House Access

The Staycation Package currently includes one-time access to each of:

- Jet Ski
- Speed Boat
- Pontoon Boat

These are separate Boat House experiences with their own capacity, safety, participation, and operating requirements.

Additional or repeated access should not be assumed as part of the standard one-time entitlement.
