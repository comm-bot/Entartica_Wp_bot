# Water Bike — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Water Bike at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is an H2O Play Park activity that combines cycling-style movement with the experience of travelling across the water.

Guests actively participate by cycling on the water while enjoying the surrounding scenery. Entartica positions the experience as a combination of recreation, gentle physical activity, adventure, and relaxation rather than a high-speed thrill ride.

It is a good option for guests who want to stay active while enjoying the lake environment at an easier pace.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Combines cycling-style activity with being on the water.
- Guests actively participate rather than riding only as passengers.
- Provides a gentle workout during the experience.
- Allows guests to enjoy the surrounding scenery while moving across the water.
- Combines light adventure with a more relaxed pace.
- Offers a different experience from high-speed motorised rides.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests can expect to participate in a cycling-style activity on the water.

The experience focuses on movement, scenery, and gentle physical participation rather than high speed.

Guests must follow the applicable safety and operating instructions provided by Entartica staff.
