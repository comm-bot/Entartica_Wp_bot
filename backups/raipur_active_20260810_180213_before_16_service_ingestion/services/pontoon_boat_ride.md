# Pontoon Boat Ride — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Pontoon Boat Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a relaxed group boat experience designed for guests who want to enjoy the water and surrounding views together at an easy pace.

Rather than focusing on speed or high-energy thrills, the Pontoon Boat offers a spacious and comfortable setting where families and groups can sit back, enjoy the surroundings, and spend time together on the water.

Entartica positions the Pontoon Boat as a smooth, group-friendly ride that works well for family outings, friends, small groups, and guests looking for a calmer alternative to high-speed water experiences.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Offers a smooth and relaxed group boat experience.
- Provides a comfortable way to enjoy the lake surroundings.
- Allows families and friends to experience the water together.
- Requires little physical participation from guests.
- An experienced captain handles navigation.
- Offers a calmer alternative to high-speed rides such as Jet Ski and Speed Boat.
- The spacious format makes it suitable for group sightseeing and leisure.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests can expect a comfortable group ride aboard the Pontoon Boat while an experienced captain navigates the water.

The experience focuses on relaxation, sightseeing, conversation, and spending time together rather than speed or active guest participation.

Guests must follow all applicable safety instructions provided by Entartica staff during boarding, the ride, and disembarkation.

## Capacity

Current official Entartica Pontoon Boat information states:

**Up to 12 guests per boat**

This is the current published Pontoon Boat configuration.

## Duration

Typical ride duration:

**Approximately 5 to 10 minutes**

This is the approved duration for the Pontoon Boat Ride as a one-time access experience.

Current Entartica website material also publishes a 30-minute Pontoon Boat configuration. That website value does not replace the approved 5–10 minute ride duration for the Raipur chatbot knowledge base.

## Operating Hours

## Access Type

**One-Time Access**

The Pontoon Boat Ride is a one-time Boat House experience.

One-time access refers to one ride/session under the applicable access arrangement and does not mean unlimited repeat use.
