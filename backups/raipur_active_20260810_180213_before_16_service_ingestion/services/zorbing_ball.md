# Zorbing Ball — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Zorbing Ball at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is an interactive H2O Play Park activity where guests enter a giant ball and enjoy moving across the surface of the water.

The experience is built around trying to walk, roll, and bounce while inside the ball, creating a playful challenge where staying balanced becomes part of the fun.

Entartica positions Zorbing Ball as an energetic and laughter-focused activity rather than a conventional boat ride. It is a good option for guests looking for something unusual, playful, and physically interactive on the water.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Guests experience being inside a giant ball on the water.
- Walking and trying to stay balanced creates an interactive challenge.
- Rolling and bouncing make the activity playful and unpredictable.
- The experience encourages active participation rather than passive riding.
- Entartica positions it around fun, laughter, and unusual water-based movement.
- Provides a very different experience from boats, pedal activities, and high-speed motorised rides.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests can expect to enter a giant Zorbing Ball and experience movement across the water.

The activity can involve:

- Walking
- Rolling
- Bouncing
- Balancing

The changing movement of the ball makes balance part of the experience and contributes to its playful character.

Guests must follow all applicable operating and safety instructions provided by Entartica staff.
