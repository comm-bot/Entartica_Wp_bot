# Pontoon Celebration — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Pontoon Celebration at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is an on-water celebration experience designed for birthdays, anniversaries, romantic dates, marriage proposals, and other intimate special occasions.

Guests celebrate aboard a decorated Pontoon Boat while enjoying the surrounding water, music, cake, and dedicated celebration moments. The experience combines the relaxed character of a Pontoon Boat with an occasion-focused setup, making it different from the standard Pontoon Boat Ride.

Entartica currently positions Pontoon Celebration as a peaceful and elegant option for small celebrations where guests want to enjoy their occasion together on the water rather than at a conventional venue.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Turns the Pontoon Boat into a dedicated celebration setting.
- Allows a small group to celebrate together on the water.
- Red-carpet entry and cold pyros create a distinctive arrival moment.
- Music adds atmosphere during the celebration.
- Cake and fruit champagne add dedicated celebration elements.
- The water surroundings provide a scenic setting for birthdays, anniversaries, romantic dates, and proposals.
- Personalised décor, dining, and photography options can further tailor selected celebrations.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests can expect to step onto a decorated Pontoon Boat with celebration elements including a red-carpet welcome, music, cold pyros, cake, and fruit champagne.

The experience is designed around spending time together in a small-group water setting rather than taking a standard sightseeing ride.

The current official information also confirms that an onboard music system is available and guests can play their own music through Bluetooth.

Food is not a standard confirmed inclusion. Dining arrangements may be available separately as an optional enhancement.
