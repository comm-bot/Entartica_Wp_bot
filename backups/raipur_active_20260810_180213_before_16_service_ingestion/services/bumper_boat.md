# Bumper Boat — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Experience Overview

The Bumper Boat at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is an interactive H2O Play Park activity built around steering, playful bumping, dodging, and enjoying time on the water with other participants.

Instead of sitting back as a passenger, guests take an active role in the experience by controlling the bumper boat themselves. The Adult Bumper Boat is designed around friendly competition and splash-filled fun, making it a more interactive experience than a sightseeing or relaxed cruising activity.

Entartica offers both **Adult Bumper Boat** and **Kids Bumper Boat** at Raipur. The Adult version is designed for older participants, while the Kids Bumper Boat is a smaller child-focused version where younger guests can enjoy steering and playful bumping.

## What Makes This Experience Special

- Guests actively control the bumper boat rather than riding only as passengers.
- Steering and dodging make the experience interactive throughout.
- Bumping other boats creates playful interaction between participants.
- The Adult Bumper Boat can be enjoyed by two people together.
- No previous operating experience is required for the Adult Bumper Boat.
- Entartica describes the Adult Bumper Boat controls as easy and user-friendly for first-time riders.
- A separate Kids Bumper Boat gives children their own child-focused version of the activity.

## What Guests Can Expect

### Adult Bumper Boat

Guests take control of their bumper boat and manoeuvre around the activity area while interacting with other participating boats.

The experience focuses on steering, dodging, playful bumping, and splashing. It is designed as an active and social water activity rather than a calm sightseeing ride.

No previous experience is required to operate the Adult Bumper Boat.

### Kids Bumper Boat

The Kids Bumper Boat is a smaller bumper-boat experience designed specifically for children.

Children can steer the kid-sized boats and enjoy playful movement and bumping on the water.

Detailed Kids Bumper Boat age, capacity, weight, and session rules are not currently established in this knowledge document.
