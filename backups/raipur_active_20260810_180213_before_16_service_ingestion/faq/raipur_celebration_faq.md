# Celebration FAQ

## Which celebration options can I enquire about?

Entartica Sea World, Raipur accepts enquiries for Pontoon Celebration, Floating Gazebo, Jetty Gazebo, Houseboat Celebration, and Party Boat Celebration. The Entartica team can help confirm the suitable option for your occasion.

## Can I get a price, availability, or a confirmed booking?

Current prices, availability, package inclusions, and final booking confirmation must be confirmed by the Entartica team. Sharing details in chat is an enquiry, not a confirmed booking.

## Can I request a custom arrangement?

Please share your celebration requirements as an enquiry. Decoration, food, music, photography, accessibility, payment, cancellation, refund, and any other special arrangement require direct confirmation by the Entartica team.
