# Pontoon Celebration — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Pontoon Celebration at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a private floating celebration charter on the flat-deck of a Pontoon Boat on Jhanjh Lake. Designed as a customisable event charter, it is used for birthdays, anniversaries, marriage proposals, romantic dates, and corporate milestone events. Decoration, catering, and music add-ons may be arranged in advance through the Entartica team.

## Experience Type

Private celebration charter on a moving vessel. The boat is navigated by an Entartica captain. Guests celebrate on the flat-deck during the cruise.

## General Experience

Guests board the Pontoon Boat for a private charter on Jhanjh Lake. The flat-deck provides space for celebration seating, decoration, and event activities during the cruise. Custom decoration, cake arrangements, food platters, and music may be arranged in advance as add-on options. The charter duration and available inclusions depend on the package selected and must be confirmed with the Entartica team at the time of booking.

## Suitable For

Groups celebrating birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, romantic dates, corporate events, and family milestone occasions. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience.

## Key Characteristics

- Private Pontoon Boat charter on Jhanjh Lake
- Customisable celebration event during the cruise
- Add-on options may include decoration, cake table, welcome refreshments, music, and food platters
- Advance booking of at least 24 to 48 hours generally required to coordinate arrangements
- Life jackets typically provided and required during the cruise
- No alcohol permitted on board

## How It Generally Works

1. Guest submits a celebration enquiry including the occasion, preferred date, time, and guest count.
2. Entartica team confirms date, slot, and vessel availability.
3. Custom requirements are discussed and confirmed at least 24 to 48 hours before the event.
4. Pricing and final inclusions are confirmed through the approved Entartica process.
5. Guests arrive at the designated boarding area on the event day.
6. Life jackets are provided and guests board the Pontoon Boat.
7. The captain navigates the charter on Jhanjh Lake for the booked duration.
8. Guests disembark after the session.

## Safety and Participation

All guests are generally required to wear life jackets during the cruise. The boat is navigated by an Entartica captain. No open flames, hazardous pyrotechnics, or alcohol are permitted on board. Battery-operated LED candles may be available as part of decoration options. Guests must follow all staff instructions. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability.
