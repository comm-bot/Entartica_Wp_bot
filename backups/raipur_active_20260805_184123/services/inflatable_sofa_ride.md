# Inflatable Sofa Ride — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Inflatable Sofa Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a towable water activity on Jhanjh Lake. Riders sit on a large inflatable sofa with high side walls and heavy-duty grab handles. The sofa is towed across the lake by a motorboat operated by an Entartica captain. As the boat executes turns and increases speed, the sofa bounces, drifts, and creates high-speed water splashes.

## Experience Type

Passenger tow activity. Guests ride on the inflatable sofa and do not operate any vessel. The tow boat is driven by an Entartica captain.

## General Experience

Riders sit on the inflatable sofa and hold the built-in grab handles as the captain tows the sofa across Jhanjh Lake. The boat typically executes sharp turns and builds speed, causing the sofa to bounce over waves and slide sideways. Getting wet is a normal and expected part of the experience. A safety team generally monitors the riding zone. Sessions generally last around 5 to 10 minutes, depending on the operating schedule.

## Suitable For

Groups of friends and families seeking a fast-paced shared water experience. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Guests who are pregnant or who have severe heart conditions, back or neck injuries, or a history of recent surgery should consult Entartica staff before booking.

## Key Characteristics

- Tow activity on Jhanjh Lake — captain operates the motorboat
- High side walls and grab handles for rider security during turns
- Group seating — multiple riders per session, depending on the tube model
- Getting wet is an expected part of the experience
- Life jackets typically provided and required
- Session duration generally 5 to 10 minutes

## How It Generally Works

1. Guest reports to the Inflatable Sofa zone and checks in with Entartica staff.
2. Staff provide a brief safety orientation.
3. Guests are fitted with life jackets.
4. Guests board the inflatable sofa and hold the grab handles securely.
5. The captain tows the sofa across Jhanjh Lake for 5 to 10 minutes.
6. The boat returns to the dock at the end of the session.

## Safety and Participation

All riders are generally required to wear life jackets throughout the session. The tow boat is operated by an Entartica captain. A life-safety team typically monitors the riding zone. Guests must follow all staff instructions. The activity involves high-speed towing, wave bounces, and sudden directional changes, and may not be suitable for guests with certain health conditions. Getting wet is expected. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability.

## Swimming Requirement

Swimming ability is generally not required. Riders are typically fitted with life jackets before the session begins. Current requirements must be confirmed with the Entartica team.

## Access Type

One-Time Access.

## Duration

Typical ride duration: approximately 5 to 10 minutes.

## Capacity

The inflatable sofa typically accommodates 3 to 6 riders per session, depending on the tube model. Current capacity must be confirmed before booking.

## Operating Hours

The Inflatable Sofa Ride generally operates between 10:00 AM and 6:30 PM, subject to weather and operational conditions. Current hours must be confirmed with the Entartica team.

## Comparison with Similar Experiences

The **Banana Boat Ride** is a similar towed group experience on an inflatable banana-shaped tube. The main difference is the seating style: the Inflatable Sofa has a sofa-like design with high side walls, while the Banana Boat has a straddle seating arrangement. Both are towed at speed on Jhanjh Lake. The **Speed Boat Ride** is a passenger experience in the boat itself rather than being towed behind it.
