# Kayaking — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

Kayaking at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a self-paddled water sport on Jhanjh Lake. Guests sit in an open-top sit-on-top kayak and navigate the lake using a double-bladed paddle. Single (solo) and tandem (two-person) kayak options are typically available. A dockside briefing on basic paddle technique is generally provided before launch.

## Experience Type

Self-propelled non-motorised watercraft. Guests paddle and steer independently at their own pace.

## General Experience

After a dockside orientation on how to hold the paddle, steer, and propel the kayak, guests are fitted with life jackets and launch onto the designated paddling zone on Jhanjh Lake. Guests explore at their own pace. Light water splashing from the double-bladed paddle is a normal part of paddling. A water safety team generally monitors the zone. Sessions generally last around 5 to 10 minutes, depending on the operating schedule.

## Suitable For

Solo paddlers, couples, and guests who enjoy a calm, active water experience. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Kayaking is generally not recommended for guests with acute shoulder or back injuries due to the paddling motion.

## How It Generally Works

1. Guest reports to the kayaking dock and checks in with Entartica staff.
2. Staff provide a dockside orientation on paddle technique and steering.
3. Guest is fitted with a life jacket.
4. Guest launches onto the designated paddling zone on Jhanjh Lake.
5. Guest paddles within the zone for approximately 5 to 10 minutes.
6. Guest returns to the dock at the end of the session.

## Safety and Participation

Guests are generally required to wear life jackets throughout the session. A water safety team typically monitors the designated paddling zone. Guests must follow all staff instructions. Kayaking is generally not recommended for guests with acute shoulder or back injuries. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability.

## Swimming Requirement

Swimming ability is generally not required. Guests are typically fitted with life jackets before entering the water. Current requirements must be confirmed with the Entartica team.
