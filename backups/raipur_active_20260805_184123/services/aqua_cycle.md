# Aqua Cycle — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Aqua Cycle at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a human-powered water vehicle on Jhanjh Lake. It features large buoyancy wheels and bicycle-style pedals. Guests propel themselves across the lake water at their own pace using pedals and handlebar steering. The Aqua Cycle is a non-motorised activity.

## Experience Type

Self-propelled non-motorised water vehicle. Guests pedal and steer at their own pace. No engine is involved.

## General Experience

After a brief orientation from staff, guests mount the Aqua Cycle, are fitted with a life jacket, and pedal across Jhanjh Lake within the designated zone. The large buoyancy wheels provide a stable platform. The pace is fully controlled by the rider. A water safety team generally monitors the zone. Sessions generally last around 5 to 10 minutes, depending on the operating schedule.

## Suitable For

Individuals, couples, and families looking for a relaxed, low-impact water activity. Solo and twin-seater options are typically available. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Riders generally need to be able to reach the pedals comfortably.

## How It Generally Works

1. Guest reports to the Aqua Cycle zone and checks in with Entartica staff.
2. Staff provide a brief orientation.
3. Guest is fitted with a life jacket.
4. Guest mounts the Aqua Cycle and pedals within the designated lake zone.
5. Session ends after approximately 5 to 10 minutes and guest returns to the dock.

## Safety and Participation

Guests are generally required to wear life jackets throughout the session. A water safety team typically monitors the activity zone. Guests must follow all staff instructions. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability. Guests with health concerns should consult Entartica staff before participating.
