# Kids Bumper Boat

## Overview

Kids Bumper Boat is a child-friendly water activity where children can steer small bumper boats within a controlled activity area and enjoy gentle interaction with other boats.

## Access Type

Included under H2O Playpark full-day access.

## H2O Playpark Access Hours

H2O Playpark access is available from 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM.

This timing refers to the H2O Playpark access window and does not mean that one Kids Bumper Boat session continues for the entire day.

## Suitable For

Designed as a recreational activity for children, subject to the on-site operator’s safety assessment and participation rules.

## Safety and Eligibility

Children must follow the activity operator’s instructions.

Guardian supervision, individual eligibility, session timing, live availability, and any special requirements must be confirmed by the Entartica team.
