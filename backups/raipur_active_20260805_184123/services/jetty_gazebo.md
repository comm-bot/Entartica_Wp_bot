# Jetty Gazebo — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Jetty Gazebo at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a semi-floating, shaded waterfront gazebo located at the end of the main pier overlooking Jhanjh Lake. Built on a solid, non-moving pier structure, it is accessed directly from the shore — no boat or water transfer is required. It is designed for group celebrations including birthdays, lakeside dining events, corporate gatherings, and waterfront anniversary occasions.

## Experience Type

Shore-accessible private celebration venue on the pier. No water transfer required. The gazebo is on a solid, non-moving structure connected to land.

## General Experience

Guests access the Jetty Gazebo directly from the shore via the main pier. The structure is covered with a shaded roof and offers views of Jhanjh Lake. Custom decoration, multi-course dining, and music may be set up in advance through the Entartica team as add-on options. Advance booking of at least 24 to 48 hours is generally required. The solid pier structure means no rocking or water movement during the event.

## Suitable For

Groups celebrating birthdays, lakeside dining gatherings, corporate team events, waterfront anniversary dinners, and family celebrations with lake views. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. The pier structure is directly accessible from the shore.
