# Speed Boat Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Overview

The Speed Boat Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a high-speed multi-passenger motorboat experience on Jhanjh Lake. The boat is operated exclusively by a NIWS-certified captain — guests ride as passengers. The boat travels at high speed, making sharp turns and creating wave splashes. No prior experience or swimming ability is required.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests are fitted with mandatory high-buoyancy life jackets before boarding. The NIWS-certified captain navigates the boat at high speed across Jhanjh Lake, including controlled turns and fast movement. Guests experience the ride as passengers. The session lasts 5 to 10 minutes, then the boat returns to the dock.

## Suitable For

Guests who enjoy a high-speed water experience as passengers. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Not recommended for guests who are pregnant or who have severe back or neck injuries or critical heart conditions.

## Key Features

- Operated exclusively by a NIWS-certified captain — guests are passengers only
- High-speed ride on Jhanjh Lake with sharp turns and wave splashes
- Multi-passenger boat suitable for groups and families
- High-buoyancy life jackets provided and mandatory
- Water safety escorts on standby during the session
- Duration: 5 to 10 minutes

## How It Works

1. Arrive at the Speed Boat zone and check in with Entartica staff.
2. Be fitted with a mandatory high-buoyancy life jacket.
3. Board the speed boat with your group.
4. The NIWS-certified captain navigates the boat at high speed across Jhanjh Lake.
5. The ride lasts 5 to 10 minutes, then the boat returns to the dock.
6. Disembark and return safety equipment.

## Safety Information

All passengers must wear mandatory high-buoyancy life jackets throughout the session. The boat is operated only by a NIWS-certified captain. Water safety escorts are on standby. Guests must follow all instructions from the captain and Entartica staff. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability.

## Swimming Requirement

Swimming ability is not required. All passengers are provided with mandatory high-buoyancy life jackets before boarding.

## Participation Requirements
