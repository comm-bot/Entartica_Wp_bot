# Entartica Sea World, Raipur — General Information

## About Entartica Sea World, Raipur

Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a water-based activity and celebration destination located at Mayfair Lake Resort on the banks of Jhanjh Lake, Naya Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India. The venue offers a range of motorised water sports, non-motorised water activities, leisure boat rides, floating celebration experiences, and resort packages — all set on and around Jhanjh Lake.

Entartica Sea World, Raipur is operated by Entartica Sea World in association with Mayfair Lake Resort.

All water activities take place on or around Jhanjh Lake at this location. Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a separate location from any other Entartica Sea World venue. Information in this knowledge base applies only to the Raipur location.

## Activities and Experiences

Entartica Sea World, Raipur offers experiences across three broad categories. Current availability of each activity must be confirmed with the Entartica team before visiting.

### Water Sports

Self-driven, passenger, and towed motorised activities on Jhanjh Lake:

- **Jet Ski Ride** — Self-driven personal watercraft. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Speed Boat Ride** — High-speed passenger motorboat operated by an Entartica captain. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Flyboard Experience** — Instructor-guided hydro-jet elevation above the lake. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes of active ride time.
- **Inflatable Sofa Ride** — Towed high-speed group activity on a plush inflatable sofa. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Banana Boat Ride** — Group tow activity on an inflatable banana-shaped tube. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.

### Non-Motorised Water Activities

Human-powered and self-propelled activities on Jhanjh Lake:

- **Aqua Cycle** — Pedal-powered water vehicle with large buoyancy wheels. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Water Bike** — Bicycle frame on dual pontoon floats. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Kayaking** — Self-paddled open-top kayak, solo or tandem. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Bumper Boat** — Self-driven motorised bumper boats in an enclosed arena (Adult and Kids variants). Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Kids Paddle Boat** — Child-sized pedal boat in a supervised calm-water zone. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Aqua Roller** — Sealed transparent cylindrical inflatable on the lake surface with safety tether. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.
- **Zorbing Ball** — Sealed transparent spherical inflatable on the lake surface with safety tether.
- **Pontoon Boat Ride** — Flat-decked group leisure cruise with canopy shade. Sessions generally 5 to 10 minutes.

### Celebration Experiences

Private and group event experiences on and around Jhanjh Lake:

- **Party Boat Celebration** — Large-group floating celebration on a lounge vessel. Up to 50 guests. Duration: 2 hours (starting).
- **Pontoon Celebration** — Private celebration charter on a Pontoon Boat. Up to 10 to 15 guests typically.
- **Floating Gazebo** — Stationary private luxury structure anchored on Jhanjh Lake. Intimate groups typically up to 6 to 8 guests.
- **Jetty Gazebo** — Shaded waterfront gazebo on the main pier with shore access. Groups typically up to 15 to 20 guests.
- **Houseboat Celebration** — Premium private floating vessel for couples and small intimate events.

### Resort Packages

Combined packages bundling resort accommodation or day-use with water activities:

- **Staycation Combo** — 1-night stay at Mayfair Lake Resort with unlimited H2O Play Park access and one-time boat house pass.
- **Daycation Package** — Full-day package (generally 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM) with day-use Club Room access, unlimited H2O Play Park access, one-time boat house pass, and food voucher.

## General Visitor Information

### Life Jackets
High-buoyancy life jackets are generally provided on-site for all water activities and are required for all participants. Life jackets must be worn before entering the water or boarding any vessel.

### Instructors and Captains
All instructors and captains at Entartica Sea World, Raipur are NIWS-certified (National Institute of Water Sports). Safety briefings are generally provided before motorised and guided activities.

### Swimming Ability
Swimming ability is generally not required for any activity at Entartica Sea World, Raipur. Guests are typically fitted with life jackets before each water activity. Current participation requirements must be confirmed for each activity before booking.

### Participation and Safety Requirements
Participation in all activities is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Guests with health concerns — including pregnancy, heart conditions, back or neck injuries, respiratory conditions, claustrophobia, or a history of recent surgery — should consult Entartica staff before booking any activity. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before each experience.

### Weather and Availability
All activities are subject to weather and operational conditions. Activities may be suspended during adverse weather including high winds and storms. Guests should confirm activity availability before visiting and on the day of their visit.

### Dress Code
- **Water sports:** Swimwear, rashguards, or quick-drying synthetic activewear is generally recommended.
- **Leisure rides and celebrations:** Casual attire, smart casuals, or appropriate occasion wear.
- **Aqua Roller and Zorbing Ball:** Comfortable casual wear; footwear must be removed before entry.

### Prohibited Items (General)
- Open alcoholic beverages in the activity zones
- Open food and drinks on most watercraft
- Weapons and hazardous items
- Smoking and tobacco products on vessels
- Loose footwear on motorised watercraft
- Unsecured electronic devices on watercraft without waterproof protection
- Pets on vessels

## Celebrations and Events

Entartica Sea World, Raipur offers several celebration experiences suited for birthdays, anniversaries, marriage proposals, corporate events, kitty parties, pre-wedding functions, and romantic occasions. Each celebration experience has its own capacity, duration, inclusions, and advance booking requirements.

Custom decoration, food, music, and other arrangements are typically available as add-on options for celebration experiences and must be confirmed in advance with the Entartica team. All celebration add-ons must be discussed and confirmed before the event date.

Advance booking is generally required for all celebration experiences — at least 24 to 48 hours for most, and at least 48 hours for the Floating Gazebo due to custom culinary and decoration preparation.
