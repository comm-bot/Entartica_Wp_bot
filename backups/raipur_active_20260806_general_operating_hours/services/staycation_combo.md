# Staycation Combo — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Staycation Combo at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is an overnight resort package combining a luxury stay at Mayfair Lake Resort with access to Entartica Sea World's water park rides and motorised boat activities on Jhanjh Lake. It is designed for couples, families, and guests seeking an overnight getaway with water adventure. The package typically includes accommodation, breakfast, water park access, and one-time boat house access.

## Experience Type

Overnight resort package. Combines 1-night hotel accommodation with water activity access. Not a standalone water sport — it is a bundled package.

## General Experience

Guests check in to a Plush Club Room at Mayfair Lake Resort and have access to the resort's facilities during their stay. Water park access covers the H2O Play Park rides on a typically unlimited basis. One-time individual passes for selected motorised boat activities are generally included. A complimentary breakfast for two adults is typically included. A spa services discount at the resort may also be available as part of the package.

## Suitable For

Couples, families, and leisure travellers who want an overnight resort experience combined with water activities on Jhanjh Lake. Participation in individual water activities is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before each activity.

## Key Characteristics

- 1-night stay in a Plush Club Room at Mayfair Lake Resort with lakeview setting
- Complimentary breakfast for 2 adults typically included
- Unlimited access to H2O Play Park (non-motorised rides) during the stay
- One-time individual pass for motorised boat activities typically included
- Spa discount at Mayfair Lake Resort may be available
- Discounted add-on rates for additional signature boat rides or celebration setups
- Standard check-in and check-out timings apply (confirm with the resort)

## What Is Typically Included

### Accommodation and Dining
- 1-night stay in Plush Club Room for two adults
- Complimentary breakfast for 2 adults

### H2O Play Park — Unlimited Access (Non-Motorised)
Rides typically included on an unlimited basis during the stay:
- Kayaking
- Aqua Cycle
- Aqua Roller
- Water Bike
- Zorbing Ball

### One-Time Boat House Pass (Motorised)
One-time individual pass each typically for:
- Jet Ski Ride (generally 5 to 10 minutes per session)
- Speed Boat Ride (generally 5 to 10 minutes per session)
- Pontoon Boat Ride (generally 5 to 10 minutes per session)

### Additional Perks
- Approximately 15% discount on spa services at Mayfair Lake Resort (subject to availability)
- Discounted rates available for additional signature boat rides or private celebration setups

## Participation Requirements

Participation in individual water activities is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment for each activity. Life jackets are generally required for all water activities. A valid government photo ID is typically required at check-in. Current participation rules for each activity must be confirmed before the experience.

## Package Timing

The standard package check-in and check-out timings must be confirmed with the Entartica and resort teams. Water park activities generally operate between 10:00 AM and 6:30 PM, subject to weather and operational conditions.

## Cancellation Policy

Cancellation and refund terms must be confirmed with the Entartica team at the time of booking. Last-minute cancellations may be subject to applicable terms. Rescheduling may be possible in cases of severe weather disruptions, at the team's discretion.

## Comparison with Similar Packages

The **Daycation Package** is a same-day (no overnight stay) option, offering daytime club room access, water activity passes, and a food voucher. The Staycation Combo is the overnight version for guests who want to extend their experience.

## Confirmation Required from Entartica

Before booking, the following must be verified with the Entartica team:

- Current pricing for the package
- Date and room availability
- Exact inclusions and any exclusions
- Check-in and check-out timings
- Spa discount availability
- Activity eligibility for all guests in the party
