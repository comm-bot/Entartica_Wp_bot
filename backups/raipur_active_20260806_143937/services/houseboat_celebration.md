# Houseboat Celebration — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Houseboat Celebration at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a premium private floating vessel experience on Jhanjh Lake. The houseboat is navigated by an Entartica captain for cruising sessions or may be anchored for a stationary floating experience. It is suited to couples' experiences, anniversaries, intimate celebrations, pre-wedding shoots, and small private gatherings. Evening and overnight stay options may be available subject to prior reservation.

## Experience Type

Private floating vessel. Guests have exclusive use of the houseboat for the booked session. An Entartica captain navigates during cruising sessions.

## General Experience

Guests board the houseboat at the designated area. The vessel is navigated at a gentle, slow pace for cruising, or may remain anchored. The houseboat features a panoramic viewing deck. Food, beverage, and dining options may be available through the Entartica team, subject to advance confirmation. Custom decoration setups for romantic or celebration occasions may be arranged in advance. The vessel does not have air conditioning — guests are on an open vessel on the lake.

## Suitable For

Couples, intimate groups, and guests seeking a private event or celebration on the water. Common occasions include romantic escapes, anniversaries, marriage proposals, pre-wedding shoots, mehndi functions, and small intimate gatherings. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience.

Evening and overnight stay options may also be available subject to prior reservation. Current duration options and packages must be confirmed with the Entartica team.

## Capacity

The houseboat typically accommodates up to 2 guests for intimate stays and couples' experiences. For small private events and functions, it may host a small group. Current capacity for specific booking types must be confirmed before booking.

## Operating Hours

10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, subject to weather and operational conditions.

## Onboard Environment

There is no air conditioning on the Houseboat. Guests are on an open vessel on Jhanjh Lake. Guests should dress appropriately for an outdoor water environment.

## Conduct Rules

The following are not permitted on board: weapons, illicit substances, smoking and tobacco products, outside gas or electric cooking stoves, pets, and behaviour associated with severe alcohol intoxication. Safety railings must not be sat on or leaned over.
