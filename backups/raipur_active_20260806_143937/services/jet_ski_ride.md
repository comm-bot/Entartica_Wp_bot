# Jet Ski Ride — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Jet Ski Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a self-driven personal watercraft experience on Jhanjh Lake. Guests operate a jet-powered water scooter independently, navigating across the open water within a designated riding zone. A safety briefing from an on-site instructor is typically provided before launch.

## Experience Type

Self-driven motorised watercraft. Guests control their own Jet Ski. No co-pilot or captain is present on the same craft.

## General Experience

After a safety briefing and gear fitting, riders launch onto the designated zone on Jhanjh Lake and manoeuvre the watercraft at their own pace. Sessions generally last around 5 to 10 minutes, depending on the operating schedule. The experience includes acceleration, turning, and open-water riding within the supervised zone. A safety team is generally on standby throughout the session.

## Suitable For

Guests who enjoy active, self-driven water sports. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Guests who are pregnant or who have severe heart conditions, back or neck injuries, or a history of recent surgery should consult Entartica staff before booking.

## Key Characteristics

- Self-operated single-seat watercraft on Jhanjh Lake, Raipur
- Safety briefing generally provided by an on-site instructor before each session
- Engine cut-off lanyard typically attached to the rider as a safety feature
- High-buoyancy life jacket generally provided and required for the ride
- Water safety personnel typically on standby in the riding zone
- Session duration generally 5 to 10 minutes

## How It Generally Works

1. Guest reports to the Jet Ski zone and checks in with Entartica staff.
2. Instructor typically provides a safety briefing covering controls, the engine cut-off lanyard, and riding guidelines.
3. Guest is fitted with a life jacket before entering the water.
4. Guest launches onto the designated water zone and completes the riding circuit.
5. Session ends when the allocated time is complete and the guest returns to the dock.
6. Staff assist with dismounting and equipment return.

## Safety and Participation

Guests must follow all instructions given by Entartica staff and instructors. The high-buoyancy life jacket must be worn throughout the ride. Each Jet Ski is typically fitted with an automatic engine cut-off lanyard — if the rider falls, the engine generally stops as a safety measure. A water safety team is generally on standby during the session. This activity involves high-speed movement on open water and may not be suitable for guests with certain health conditions. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability.

## Swimming Requirement

Swimming ability is generally not required for the Jet Ski Ride. Guests are typically fitted with a life jacket before entering the water. Current swimming and participation requirements must be confirmed with the Entartica team before booking.

## Access Type

One-Time Access.

## Duration

Typical ride duration: approximately 5 to 10 minutes.

## Capacity

Each Jet Ski typically accommodates one rider at a time, depending on the equipment model. Current equipment configuration must be confirmed before booking.

## Operating Hours

10:00 AM to 6:30 PM, subject to weather and operational conditions.

## Comparison with Similar Experiences

The **Speed Boat Ride** is a passenger experience driven by a captain — guests do not operate the vessel. The **Inflatable Sofa Ride** and **Banana Boat Ride** are towed group activities. The Jet Ski Ride is the only fully self-driven motorised watercraft experience at Entartica Sea World, Raipur.
