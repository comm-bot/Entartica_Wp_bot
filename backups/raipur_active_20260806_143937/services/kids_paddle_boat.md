# Kids Paddle Boat at Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Overview

The Kids Paddle Boat at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a child-friendly pedal boat designed specifically for young guests. The colourful, lightweight boats feature easy foot pedals or hand cranks and simple steering handles. Children navigate within a designated calm, shallow-water zone under the supervision of parents or guardians from the adjacent dock. Dedicated water safety staff monitor all children in the zone at all times. No swimming ability is required.

## What Guests Can Expect

Children board the child-sized paddle boat in the designated shallow-water zone. Parents and guardians watch from the dock area while dedicated safety staff monitor the water zone throughout the session. The boats operate at a slow, child-controlled pace within the enclosed area.

## Suitable For

Young children who enjoy a fun, active water activity in a supervised calm-water zone. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Children must be able to reach the pedals comfortably. Parents and guardians must supervise from the dock at all times.

## Key Features

- Child-sized pedal boat with simple foot pedals or hand cranks
- Designated calm, shallow-water enclosed safety zone
- Dedicated water safety staff monitor all children at all times
- Child-sized high-buoyancy life jackets provided and mandatory
- Wide, stable hull design for on-water stability
- Parents and guardians supervise from the adjacent dock
- Included under H2O Playpark full-day access

## How It Works

1. Arrive at the Kids Paddle Boat area with the child and check in with Entartica staff.
2. The child is fitted with a mandatory child-sized high-buoyancy life jacket.
3. The child boards the paddle boat in the designated shallow-water zone.
4. Parents and guardians remain at the dock to supervise.
5. Dedicated safety staff monitor all children throughout the session.
6. Staff assist with safe return and disembarkation at the end of the session.

## Safety Information

All Kids Paddle Boat participants must wear mandatory child-sized high-buoyancy life jackets throughout the session. The activity takes place in a designated calm, enclosed shallow-water safety zone. Dedicated water safety staff actively monitor all children on the water at all times. Parents and guardians must supervise their children from the adjacent dock area throughout the session. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability.
