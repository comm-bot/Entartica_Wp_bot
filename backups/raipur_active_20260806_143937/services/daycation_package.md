# Daycation Package — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Daycation Package at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a full-day experience combining daytime resort room access with unlimited water park activity access and one-time boat house passes. No overnight stay is required. It is designed for families, groups, couples, and day visitors who want a complete water adventure with resort facilities for the day. The package typically runs from 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## Experience Type

Daytime resort package. Combines day-use club room access with water activity passes. Not a standalone water sport — it is a bundled same-day package.

## General Experience

Guests receive access to a private Club Room at Mayfair Lake Resort for the full daycation window — for changing, resting, and relaxing between activities. The package typically includes unlimited access to H2O Play Park non-motorised rides and a one-time individual pass for three selected motorised boat activities. A food voucher redeemable at the partnered on-site restaurant during a specified window is also typically included.

## Suitable For

Families, friend groups, couples, and day visitors who want a full-day water activity experience combined with resort comfort without an overnight stay. Participation in individual water activities is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before each activity.

## Key Characteristics

- Day-use access to a Plush Club Room at Mayfair Lake Resort for up to 4 guests
- Unlimited H2O Play Park non-motorised ride access during the package window
- One-time individual pass for selected motorised boat activities
- Food voucher redeemable at the partnered restaurant during the afternoon window
- Package window generally 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM (subject to confirmation)
- No overnight stay included

## What Is Typically Included

### Resort and Relaxation
- Day-use access to a Plush Club Room for up to 4 guests (for changing, resting, and relaxing)
- Food voucher redeemable at the on-site Chowpatty restaurant during the afternoon window

### H2O Play Park — Unlimited Access (Non-Motorised)
Rides typically included on an unlimited basis during the package:
- Kayaking
- Aqua Cycle
- Aqua Roller
- Water Bike
- Adult Bumper Boat
- Kids Pedal Boat
- Zorbing Ball

### One-Time Boat House Pass (Motorised)
One-time individual pass each typically for:
- Jet Ski Ride (generally 5 to 10 minutes per session)
- Speed Boat Ride (generally 5 to 10 minutes per session)
- Pontoon Boat Cruise (generally 5 to 10 minutes per session)

### Add-On Perks
- Discounted rates may be available for additional signature boat rides or celebration setups (subject to confirmation)

## Guest Limit

The base package typically covers up to 4 guests. Additional guests may be added at applicable extra charges. Confirm with the Entartica team.

## Participation Requirements

Participation in individual water activities is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment for each activity. Life jackets are generally required for all water activities. A valid government photo ID is typically required at check-in. Swimwear or quick-drying clothing is generally required for water sports.

## Package Timing

The Daycation Package window is generally 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Individual water ride activities generally operate between 10:00 AM and 6:30 PM, subject to weather and operational conditions. Current timings must be confirmed with the Entartica team.

## Cancellation Policy

Cancellation and refund terms must be confirmed with the Entartica team at the time of booking. In cases of severe weather disruptions, rescheduling may be possible at the team's discretion.

## Comparison with Similar Packages

The **Staycation Combo** is the overnight version of this package, including a 1-night hotel stay and breakfast. The Daycation Package is designed for guests who do not want an overnight stay but want a full day of resort and water activity access.

## Confirmation Required from Entartica
