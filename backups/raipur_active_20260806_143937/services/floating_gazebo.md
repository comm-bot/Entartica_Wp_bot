# Floating Gazebo — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Floating Gazebo at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a private, stationary luxury structure anchored on Jhanjh Lake. Unlike a moving boat, it remains in a fixed position on the water. It is designed for intimate, high-end occasions such as romantic dinners, marriage proposals, milestone birthdays, and exclusive anniversary celebrations for couples and small groups.

## Experience Type

Stationary private venue on the water. Guests are transported to the anchored gazebo and celebrate in a fixed lakeside setting. The gazebo does not cruise or move.

## General Experience

Guests are transferred to the Floating Gazebo by a dock-shuttle arrangement. The gazebo is anchored on Jhanjh Lake and features high protective side railings and a covered canopy roof. As a stationary structure, it provides a calm, stable setting on the water. Custom dining, decoration, and lighting may be arranged in advance through the Entartica team as add-on options. Advance booking of at least 48 hours is generally required. Safety escorts are typically on standby via dock-shuttle during the booking.

## Suitable For

Couples, small groups, and guests seeking an intimate private celebration on the water. Common occasions include romantic candlelight dinners, marriage proposals, and milestone anniversary or birthday events. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience.
