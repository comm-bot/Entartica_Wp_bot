# Pontoon Boat Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Overview

The Pontoon Boat Ride at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a relaxed group leisure cruise on Jhanjh Lake. The flat-decked vessel has comfortable lounge seating and an overhead canopy for shade. The boat is professionally navigated by a NIWS-certified captain. It is designed for large families, corporate teams, and friend groups who want a calm, scenic group outing on the lake. No prior experience or swimming ability is required.

## What Guests Can Expect

Guests board the Pontoon Boat and take their seats on the flat-decked vessel with shade from the overhead canopy. The NIWS-certified captain navigates the boat at a smooth, gentle pace across Jhanjh Lake. High perimeter safety railings enclose the seating area. The experience is calm and social with minimal physical activity required. Getting wet is not expected. The ride lasts 5 to 10 minutes.

## Suitable For

Large families, multi-generational groups, corporate teams, and guests of all fitness levels who prefer a relaxed water experience. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Children must be accompanied by an adult.

## Key Features

- Professionally navigated by a NIWS-certified captain
- Flat-decked vessel with comfortable lounge seating and overhead shade canopy
- High perimeter safety railings enclosing the entire seating area
- Suitable for large groups of 8 to 15 passengers
- No splashing expected — smooth, gentle ride
- High-buoyancy life jackets provided and mandatory

## How It Works

1. Arrive at the Pontoon Boat boarding area and check in with Entartica staff.
2. Board the Pontoon Boat and take a seat.
3. All passengers are fitted with mandatory high-buoyancy life jackets.
4. The NIWS-certified captain navigates the boat on a smooth cruise across Jhanjh Lake.
5. The ride lasts 5 to 10 minutes, then the boat returns to the dock.
6. Guests disembark at the end of the session.

## Safety Information

All passengers must wear mandatory high-buoyancy life jackets throughout the session. The boat is navigated exclusively by a NIWS-certified captain. High perimeter safety railings enclose the seating area. A safety team is on standby across the lake. Guests must follow all instructions from the captain and Entartica staff. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability.
