# Zorbing Ball

## Overview

Zorbing Ball is a recreational activity where a participant enters a large transparent inflatable ball and enjoys controlled movement inside a designated activity area.

## Access Type

Included under H2O Playpark full-day access.

## Duration

This activity is included in H2O Playpark full-day access from 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM.

The access window does not mean one continuous activity session. Individual turn or session duration is not separately confirmed.

## Operating Hours

H2O Playpark access is available from 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM, subject to weather and operational conditions.

## Suitable For

Suitable for guests looking for a fun and light recreational activity, subject to the on-site operator’s safety assessment.

## Safety and Eligibility

Participants must follow the instructions of the activity operator.

Final participation eligibility, session timing, live availability, and any special requirements must be confirmed by the Entartica team.
