# Bumper Boat — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Bumper Boat at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a self-driven motorised watercraft activity in a designated enclosed arena zone. Guests drive circular inflatable boats with wide inflatable bumper rings and manoeuvre around other boats in the arena. Two variants are typically available: the Adult Bumper Boat for adults in a standard arena, and the Kids Bumper Boat for young guests in a separate shallow-water arena.

## Experience Type

Self-driven motorised bumper activity in an enclosed arena. Guests operate the boat independently using onboard steering controls.

## Adult Bumper Boat

### General Experience

Adult guests drive their own motorised circular bumper boat around a dedicated enclosed water arena using joystick or wheel controls. Wide inflatable bumper rings cushion collisions between boats. Light to moderate water splashing commonly occurs during the session. Dock operators generally manage an emergency power cut-off for the arena.

### Suitable For

Adults and groups who enjoy an interactive, playful water activity. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Guests with severe chronic back or neck injuries are generally advised not to participate due to bumping impacts. Current participation rules must be confirmed before the experience.

### Key Characteristics

- Self-driven motorised circular boat in an enclosed arena on Jhanjh Lake
- Wide inflatable bumper rings to cushion collisions between boats
- Single-seater and twin-seater options typically available, depending on the equipment model
- Enclosed, monitored arena
- Dock operators generally manage emergency power cut-off
- Life jackets typically provided and required

## Kids Bumper Boat

### General Experience

The Kids Bumper Boat is a child-sized version designed for young guests in a separate dedicated shallow-water arena. Children drive miniature circular boats with soft inflatable bumper rings using simple controls. A dedicated safety team generally supervises the kids' arena throughout the session. Parents and guardians typically supervise from the dock area.

### Suitable For

Young children who enjoy an active, playful water activity in a supervised setting. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Parents and guardians generally supervise from the dock area.

## Safety and Participation

All Bumper Boat participants are generally required to wear life jackets throughout the session. Adult and Kids arenas are typically separate. Dock operators generally manage emergency power cut-off for the Adult arena. Guests must follow all staff instructions. Guests with severe chronic back or neck injuries are generally advised not to participate in the Adult Bumper Boat due to bumping impacts. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability.

## Swimming Requirement

Swimming ability is generally not required. Participants are typically fitted with life jackets before entering the arena. Current requirements must be confirmed with the Entartica team.

## Access Type

Included under H2O Playpark full-day access.

## Duration

This activity is included in H2O Playpark full-day access from 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM.

The access window does not mean one continuous activity session. Individual turn or session duration is not separately confirmed.

## Capacity

Adult Bumper Boats typically offer single-seater and twin-seater options, depending on the equipment model. Kids Bumper Boats typically accommodate one child per boat. Current capacity must be confirmed before booking.

## Operating Hours

H2O Playpark access is available from 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM, subject to weather and operational conditions.
