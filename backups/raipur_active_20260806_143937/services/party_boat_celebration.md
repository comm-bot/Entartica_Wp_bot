# Party Boat Celebration — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Party Boat Celebration at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a group floating celebration on a spacious lounge craft on Jhanjh Lake. The vessel is professionally navigated by an Entartica captain. It accommodates up to 50 guests and features an onboard sound and music system. It is designed for birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events, kitty parties, and large group gatherings. The starting duration is 2 hours.

## Experience Type

Group celebration cruise. Guests celebrate on a large floating lounge vessel. An Entartica captain navigates the boat throughout the session.

## General Experience

Guests board the Party Boat at the designated dock area. The vessel travels at a gentle, stable pace across Jhanjh Lake for the 2-hour starting session. The onboard sound and music system allows guests to play their chosen playlist. Custom balloon and floral decoration, cake-cutting arrangements, and catering options may be arranged in advance through the Entartica team as add-on options. Life jackets are provided and required during the session.

## Suitable For

Large groups celebrating together on Jhanjh Lake. Common occasions include birthday parties, anniversaries, corporate team outings, kitty parties, group social gatherings, and celebration cruises. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience.

## How It Generally Works

1. Guest submits a celebration enquiry including the occasion, preferred date, time, and guest count.
2. Entartica team confirms date, slot, and vessel availability.
3. Custom requirements are discussed and confirmed in advance.
4. Pricing and final inclusions are confirmed through the approved Entartica process.
5. Guests arrive at the designated boarding area.
6. Life jackets are provided and guests board the Party Boat.
7. The captain navigates the vessel across Jhanjh Lake for the starting duration of 2 hours.
8. Guests disembark after the session.
