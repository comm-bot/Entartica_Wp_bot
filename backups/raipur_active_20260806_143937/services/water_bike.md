# Water Bike — Entartica Sea World, Raipur

## Definition

The Water Bike at Entartica Sea World, Raipur is a non-motorised water vehicle on Jhanjh Lake. It features a standard bicycle frame mounted on dual pontoon floats. Guests propel themselves across the lake using bicycle pedals and steer with handlebars. No special balance skill is generally required — the dual-pontoon design provides a stable platform on the water.

## Experience Type

Self-propelled non-motorised watercraft. Guests pedal and steer at their own pace. No engine is involved.

## General Experience

Guests mount the Water Bike, are fitted with a life jacket, and pedal across Jhanjh Lake within the designated zone. The dual pontoon floats keep the bike stable. Twin Water Bikes — where available — allow two guests to pedal side by side. Slight water splashes near the feet may occur while pedalling. A water safety team generally monitors the zone.

## Suitable For

Individuals, couples, and families looking for a relaxed, pedal-powered lake activity. Participation is subject to the applicable safety requirements and staff assessment. Current child-participation rules must be confirmed before the experience. Riders generally need to be able to reach the pedals comfortably.

## Key Characteristics

- Non-motorised bicycle frame on dual pontoon floats on Jhanjh Lake
- Solo and twin (side-by-side) options typically available, depending on equipment
- Dual-pontoon design provides high on-water stability
- Life jackets typically provided and required
- Water safety team generally monitors the pedalling zone
- Included under H2O Playpark full-day access

## How It Generally Works

1. Guest reports to the Water Bike zone and checks in with Entartica staff.
2. Guest is fitted with a life jacket.
3. Guest mounts the Water Bike — solo or twin — and pedals within the designated zone.
4. Guest returns to the dock at the end of the session.

## Safety and Participation

Guests are generally required to wear life jackets throughout the session. A water safety team typically monitors the pedalling zone. Guests must follow all staff instructions. Weather and operational conditions may affect availability. Guests with health concerns should consult Entartica staff before participating.

## Swimming Requirement

Swimming ability is generally not required. Guests are typically fitted with life jackets before launching. Current requirements must be confirmed with the Entartica team.
